Welcome! I am Jiawen Shi.

I' am a Ph.D candidate at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. 

My research centers on Trustworthy AI. Currently, I focus on: 1) prompt injection attacks in real-world LLM applications, 2) security and privacy in AI systems (e.g., LLM Agent), and 3) vulnerabilities and robustness of large models in deployment (e.g., backdoor attack).

Previously, I received my M.S. and B.S. degrees from Central South University in 2022 and 2019, respectively.
