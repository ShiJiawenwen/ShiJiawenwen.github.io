## Research

I study **AI security in real-world LLM applications**, with a focus on **prompt injection attacks** and **LLM agent security**. My work investigates how malicious instructions embedded in untrusted external sources can manipulate LLM-based systems, including **LLM-as-a-Judge** and **tool-selection pipelines in LLM agents**. The goal of my research is to reveal security weaknesses in deployed LLM applications and provide practical insights for assessing and mitigating these risks.
